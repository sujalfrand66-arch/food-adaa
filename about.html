How It All Started
"The best food always starts with the best intentions."

Bishnoi Food Adda started in 2013 as a humble family kitchen at the corner of Suratgarh's Main Chowk. Our founder — a home cook who had mastered desi recipes through years of watching, learning, and tasting — decided to turn passion into purpose.

The first week, we served 20 families. Within a year, it was 200. Today we're proud to serve 5,000+ loyal customers — and the recipe remains the same: fresh ingredients, ground-in-house spices, and food made with genuine care.

We've never used a frozen patty. We've never used a ready-made masala. And we'll never compromise on what makes Bishnoi Food Adda special — honest, flavourful, desi food that tastes like it was made just for you.

Our Philosophy

Food as an Act of Love

We believe food is more than fuel — it's connection. It's the smell of masala in the morning, the warmth of a dal that's been slow-cooked for hours, the first bite that takes you straight home.

At Bishnoi Food Adda, every meal is an act of love — for the ingredients, for the tradition, and most importantly, for the person about to eat it.

That's why we'll never compromise. Because when you order from us, you're not just getting food — you're getting a little piece of Suratgarh's soul.
